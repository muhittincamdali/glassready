How to fix

[A]      Remove the opt-out and review the screens with Liquid Glass
         1. Delete the UIDesignRequiresCompatibility key from the Info.plist.
         2. Build with Xcode 27 and run the app on an iOS 27 simulator; the other findings in this
            report are the places to look at first.
         3. Keep the brand colour on tint (buttons, selected tab) rather than on bar backgrounds, so
            the system can draw the bars.
         https://github.com/muhittincamdali/glassready/blob/main/docs/guides/opt-out.md

[B]      Let the system draw the bar background
         1. Remove configureWithOpaqueBackground() and the backgroundColor, backgroundImage,
            backgroundEffect and shadow settings of the appearance, or apply them only before iOS 26
            with if #available(iOS 26.0, *).
         2. Move the brand colour to tintColor and the title text attributes.
         3. Check the bar over scrolling content: the scroll edge effect replaces the hairline and
            the opaque background.
         https://github.com/muhittincamdali/glassready/blob/main/docs/guides/native-ios.md

[C]      Remove the bar colour and let the bar stay translucent
         1. Remove barTintColor, isTranslucent = false and the bar background and shadow images, or
            apply them only before iOS 26 with if #available(iOS 26.0, *).
         2. Use tintColor for the brand colour of the bar's buttons.
         https://github.com/muhittincamdali/glassready/blob/main/docs/guides/native-ios.md

[D]      Decide what the blur is for
         1. Behind a popover or sheet's content: remove it and let the presentation draw its
            background.
         2. Behind a floating control or panel: use UIGlassEffect on iOS 26 and later (if
            #available(iOS 26.0, *)) and keep the blur as the fallback.
         3. Over content (a photo, a map): keep it.
         https://github.com/muhittincamdali/glassready/blob/main/docs/guides/native-ios.md

[E]      Lay out against the safe area instead of a number
         1. Constrain to safeAreaLayoutGuide (or the keyboard and bar layout guides) instead of
            adding a bar height to the insets.
         2. Do not read the tab bar's frame for layout; check the screen with the tab bar minimized
            as well.
         https://github.com/muhittincamdali/glassready/blob/main/docs/guides/native-ios.md
