How to fix

[A]      Remove the opt-out and review the screens with Liquid Glass
         1. Delete the UIDesignRequiresCompatibility key from the Info.plist.
         2. Build with Xcode 27 and run the app on an iOS 27 simulator; the other findings in this
            report are the places to look at first.
         3. Keep the brand colour on tint (buttons, selected tab) rather than on bar backgrounds, so
            the system can draw the bars.
         https://github.com/muhittincamdali/glassready/blob/main/docs/guides/opt-out.md

[B]      Decide whether the Cupertino bars should follow the system
         1. Run the app on an iOS 27 simulator next to a system app and decide whether the
            Flutter-drawn bars may look different.
         2. To follow the system, restyle the bars yourself (translucent background, floating tab
            bar) or follow flutter/flutter#170310 for framework support.
         https://github.com/muhittincamdali/glassready/blob/main/docs/guides/flutter.md

[C]      Check the native view next to the Flutter UI
         1. Open the screen with the platform view on an iOS 27 simulator and compare its controls
            with the Flutter widgets around it.
         https://github.com/muhittincamdali/glassready/blob/main/docs/guides/flutter.md
