How to fix

[A]      Remove the build setting and review the screens with Liquid Glass
         1. Delete INFOPLIST_KEY_UIDesignRequiresCompatibility from the target's build settings.
            Xcode 27.1 does not copy it into the Info.plist it generates, so it has no effect there
            in any case.
         2. Build with Xcode 27 and run the app on an iOS 27 simulator; the other findings in this
            report are the places to look at first.
         https://github.com/muhittincamdali/glassready/blob/main/docs/guides/opt-out.md

[B]      Remove the bar background modifiers
         1. Remove toolbarBackground(_:for:) and toolbarBackgroundVisibility(_:for:), or apply them
            only before iOS 26.
         2. Keep the brand colour on tint(_:) and on the content, not on the bar.
         https://github.com/muhittincamdali/glassready/blob/main/docs/guides/native-ios.md

[C]      Use the system tab bar, or attach the custom bar with safeAreaBar
         1. Where the custom bar only restyles tabs, use TabView with Tab and
            tabBarMinimizeBehavior(_:) instead.
         2. If the bar stays custom, attach it with safeAreaBar(edge:alignment:spacing:content:) so
            content under it gets the scroll edge effect, and consider glassEffect(_:in:) for its
            background.
         https://github.com/muhittincamdali/glassready/blob/main/docs/guides/native-ios.md

[D]      Remove presentationBackground for iOS 26 and later
         1. Remove presentationBackground(_:), or apply it only before iOS 26.
         2. Look at the half-height detent: the sheet is inset and the content behind it shows
            around the edges.
         https://github.com/muhittincamdali/glassready/blob/main/docs/guides/native-ios.md

[E]      Let the safe area carry the bar's height
         1. Remove the fixed height; place bottom content with safeAreaInset(edge:) or
            safeAreaBar(edge:alignment:spacing:content:).
         https://github.com/muhittincamdali/glassready/blob/main/docs/guides/native-ios.md

[F]      Write the header in title case
         1. Write the header as "Account" instead of "ACCOUNT"; the system styles it.
         https://github.com/muhittincamdali/glassready/blob/main/docs/guides/native-ios.md
